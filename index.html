About ACM

ACM TKMCE is the student chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery, the world’s largest computing society. We bring together passionate students, educators, and innovators at TKMCE to explore the frontiers of technology, share knowledge, and build a collaborative tech community. As part of ACM’s global network of over 100,000 members, we strive to uphold the highest standards in computing while fostering growth through learning, leadership, and professional development. At ACM TKMCE, we believe in creating opportunities that inspire dialogue, spark innovation, and empower the next generation of tech leaders.
